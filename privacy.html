Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy covers the Loops of Math website and the Loops of Math uploader, the program that publishes Loops of Math videos to YouTube and Instagram.

Who uses the uploader

The uploader is used only by the owner of the Loops of Math channel, to manage that channel. It is not a public app: no one else signs in to it, and it accesses no other YouTube or Google account.

What it accesses through YouTube API Services

With the channel owner's permission, the uploader uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the Loops of Math channel;
  • read the numbers YouTube reports for those videos, such as views, likes and watch time, to learn which animations people enjoy.

It does not read, collect or store any information about viewers, commenters or other YouTube users.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in token that lets the uploader act for the channel, and the numbers of the channel's own videos, are kept on our own server, which only the channel owner can reach. They are never sold, shared or given to anyone else, and they are used for nothing but the purposes above.

Removing access

The channel owner can take back the uploader's access at any time on the Google security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is removed, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the server.

Google and YouTube

The uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects no personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub's own handling of visits is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a direct message to @loopsofmath on Instagram.
