Loops of Math

Ten-second animations of mathematics: strange attractors, Fourier circles, Chladni figures, fireflies falling into step, and more. Each one is drawn by code and checked so that what it says about the math is true.

  • Instagram
  • YouTube

How the videos are made

A small program on our own server draws each animation in a browser, records it as a video and adds generated music. A person watches and approves every video before it is published.

The Loops of Math uploader

The uploader is the part of that program that publishes the approved videos to the Loops of Math YouTube channel, using the YouTube Data API, and reads that channel's own view counts to learn which animations people enjoy. It is used only by the owner of the channel, for that one channel. It is not offered to anyone else, and it does not access any other YouTube account.
